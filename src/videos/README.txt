# Legend Moments — Video Library

Drop your MP4 clips in this folder using the exact filenames below. They will
automatically appear in the "Legend Moments" gallery on `messi.html`.

- `moment-worldcup-2022.mp4` — Messi lifts the 2022 FIFA World Cup
- `moment-ballondor-8th.mp4` — Messi wins his 8th Ballon d'Or
- `moment-copa-2021.mp4` — Copa América triumph, Maracanã 2021
- `moment-ucl-final.mp4` — Champions League glory
- `moment-getafe-2007.mp4` — The Getafe solo goal, 2007
- `moment-miami-lift.mp4` — Inter Miami era / final chapter

Tip: Keep clips under ~15 MB and MP4 (H.264) for smooth playback.
